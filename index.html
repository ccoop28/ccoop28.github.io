How it works

Simple, written, fixed-price

  1. Tell me what you need

    Email me a short description of the job, your deadline and any examples you like.

  2. Get a fixed quote

    I reply with a written quote covering scope, price, timeline, revision rounds and payment schedule.

  3. Work and review

    Once you accept and pay any deposit, I start. You review the work and request changes within the agreed revision rounds.

  4. Handover

    After final payment, I hand over the finished files, site or setup.
