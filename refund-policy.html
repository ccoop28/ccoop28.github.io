Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for services provided by ChaseDigital, operated by Chase Cooper in Perth, Western Australia.

Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth)) or other laws that cannot be excluded. Where this policy conflicts with those rights, those rights apply.

1. Quotes and payment schedule

Every job is quoted at a fixed price in writing before work starts. For large jobs, payment is made in stages: a 50% deposit to start, 25% at first review, and 25% at handover. Smaller jobs are paid as stated in the quote. All card payments are processed by Stripe.

2. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel after paying a deposit but before any work has started, your deposit will be refunded in full.

3. Cancelling after work has started

  • You may cancel a project at any time by emailing chase_cooper@icloud.com.
  • Once work has started, the deposit is non-refundable, because it covers time already reserved and work already done.
  • Milestone payments for stages that have been completed (for example, the 25% payment at first review) are non-refundable.
  • If you cancel, you pay for the work completed up to the date of cancellation, as set out in the quote. If the amount you have already paid is more than the value of the work completed, the difference will be refunded.
  • On cancellation, you will receive the work completed and paid for to that point.

4. Revisions

Each quote states the number of revision rounds included. Changes beyond the included rounds, or changes to the agreed scope, are quoted separately before they are done. Requests for revisions are handled as revisions, not refunds.

5. If something goes wrong

If the work does not match what was agreed in the quote, please email me. I will work with you to fix it. If it can't be fixed, you may be entitled to a refund or other remedy, including under the Australian Consumer Law.

6. If I cancel

If I need to cancel a project for any reason other than a breach of the terms by you, I will refund any amount paid for work that has not been completed.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 10 business days. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take a few more days to appear in your account.

8. Contact

To cancel or request a refund, email chase_cooper@icloud.com with your name and project details.
