Zhala voice samples

Each one says: "Hey Chase, it's Zhala. What do you need?" Pick A, B or C and tell the team which you like.

Made with the open-source Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign model (Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign). They are original designed voices, not imitations of any real person. The live app uses Alibaba's cloud Qwen voices (default Jennifer), which sound different.

A: Brooklyn

A confident woman in her thirties with a thick, fast Brooklyn New York accent. Quick, punchy, street-smart delivery, strong New York vowels, clear and direct, slightly raspy mid-low pitch.

B: Warm New York

A warm, friendly woman in her forties with a soft, gentle New York accent. Relaxed and caring tone, smooth mid pitch, unhurried pace, sounds like a trusted friend greeting you.

C: Long Island

A sassy, playful young woman in her twenties with a strong Long Island New York accent. Bright, bubbly, a little cheeky, expressive pitch that rises and falls, upbeat pace with attitude.
