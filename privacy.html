Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information ChaseDigital, operated by Chase Cooper in Perth, Western Australia, collects and how it is used.

1. What I collect

  • Information you send by email, such as your name, email address, business details and project information.
  • Payment information, which is collected and processed by Stripe when you pay. I receive confirmation of payment and basic details (such as your name, email and amount paid), but I do not see or store your full card details.

This website has no contact forms, accounts, tracking cookies or analytics. It does not set cookies or load third-party scripts.

2. How I use it

I use your information only to reply to your enquiry, quote, deliver and invoice your project, process payments and refunds, and meet legal and tax record-keeping obligations.

3. Sharing

I do not sell or rent personal information. It is shared only with service providers needed to run the business (such as Stripe for payments and my email provider), or where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Information is kept in my email and business records and protected with reasonable security measures. I keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any legal record-keeping period. Some service providers may store data outside Australia.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it where I'm not required to keep it. I aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. Privacy requests and complaints

Email chase_cooper@icloud.com with any privacy request or complaint. I will respond within a reasonable time. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always on this page.
