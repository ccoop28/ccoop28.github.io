Usage notes

  • Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the cup in the icon mark.
  • Minimum size: horizontal lockup 120 px wide on screen and 30 mm in print. Below that, use the icon mark alone.
  • Don't recolour the ampersand outside the palette, stretch the logo, or put the full-colour logo on busy photos. Use the reversed version instead.
  • Deliverables in a real project: SVG, PNG (transparent), PDF and EPS, plus a one-page brand sheet.
